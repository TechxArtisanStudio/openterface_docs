# KeyCmd


Turn your phone or tablet into a **keyboard and mouse controller** for any computer. **KeyCmd** sends keystrokes, mouse movements, and gamepad inputs to the target over USB or Bluetooth LE — with **KeyMod** hardware, **Mini-KVM**, or **KVM-GO**.
